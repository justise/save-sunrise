Definitions

  • Resident students are children who live in an attendance area. Enrolled FTE counts who actually attends.
  • Design capacity (EDAC) = classrooms × 22.5, including portables. Planned seats (CFP) = the official plan at 20.4 students per room.
  • Busing illustration = walkers who become bus riders × average cost per rider. It is not the marginal cost of new routes.
  • UGA = King County Urban Growth Area.
