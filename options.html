Considerations
    How this tool works
    • Students are resident students from the district's projections: children who live in each attendance area.S3 Choice transfers and out-of-district students aren't included.
    • Permanent seats are the district's own planning capacity for every school, from the Capital Facilities Plan, Table 4.2.S4 The plan counts regular classrooms in permanent buildings at an average class size of 24, then holds back 15% of space for special education, support programs and teacher planning, which works out to about 20.4 students per room. Portables aren't counted.
    • Design capacity counts every classroom, including portables, at 22.5 students each, with nothing held back.S3 It's the measure the district's advisory committee (EDAC) uses, and it makes schools look emptier: Cottage Lake under Scenario 2 is 119% of permanent seats but 92% of design capacity.
    • Where students go: by default, students are split across the receiving schools so they end up as evenly full as possible, or as close to the same size if you choose "School size." That's a best case: real boundaries move whole neighborhoods. Drag the sliders to set your own split. Suggested receiving schools are nearby schools, not district boundaries.
    • Moves between schools stand in for boundary changes, programs or choice transfers. They're applied before any closure.
    • Moorlands in the district scenarios: the district hasn't published where Moorlands students would go in the data we have, so those students are left out rather than guessed at. Use "Place them myself" to try a split.
    • New schools use the seats you enter. Their running cost is the average fixed cost of the four schools the district priced; land and construction aren't counted.
    • Savings are a rough estimate: the closed school's fixed costs (the district's figures where published, otherwise their average)S1 minus added office and support staff at receiving schools from the district's staffing tiers.S12 They leave out busing, one-time and building-upkeep costs, and won't match the district's own estimates exactly.
    • Repairs avoided are corrective work scheduled for 2028–29, published for six schools.S5
